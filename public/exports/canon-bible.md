## Archive Foundation

**Status:** strong-direction

The operating contract that keeps the archive reusable across games without forcing literal lore repetition.

## What this archive is

The EGGS / Para archive is an evergreen creative systems library. It preserves symbolic pressures, thematic questions, mechanical patterns, aesthetic motifs, player identities, competitive meanings, and project-specific expressions.

It is not one finished story, one game's lore bible, or a requirement that every game reproduce the same factions literally.

> Alien principles define pressures on what can happen. Mortal expressions show how those pressures feel when embodied in a world, institution, game, or player experience.

## Reuse standard

A future game should document at least one material derivation from the archive. That derivation may be explicit lore, a visible system, an aesthetic pattern, symbolic language, or hidden mechanical logic. Copying only a name, color, or emblem is not meaningful reuse.

Projects may intentionally omit principles that do not improve the game.

## Voltari

**Status:** strong-direction

Discipline, fairness, coordination, mastery, stable memory, and dependable structures that make meaningful competition possible.

## Zynarth

**Status:** strong-direction

Pressure, appetite, multiplication, and the moment quantity becomes destiny.

## Core question

What happens when more becomes unstoppable?

Zynarth is not merely insects, monsters, or biological horror. It is the logic of accumulation: repetition, compounding advantage, crowd force, appetite, survival, and collective action.

## Constructive expression

Resilience, adaptation, solidarity, and the ability of many small actors to challenge concentrated power.

## Failure mode

## Mechanical vocabulary

Multiply, compound, consume, recover, spread, recruit, overwhelm, and overextend.
