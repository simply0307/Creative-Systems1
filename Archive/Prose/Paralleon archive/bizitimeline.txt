Bizi Timeline: The Gears of Eternity
1. Kharon’s Vision

A young inventor named Kharon receives visions from Titan Machina. He defeats Maxor the Usurper at Iron River and founds Constanti.

Bizi’s known history begins with invention, divine-machine visions, and the claim that the future will belong not to kings, but to builders.

2. The First Titan

Machina sleeps beneath Constanti. Bizi thinkers divide over whether the Titan created them or merely guides them.

This establishes the central religious and philosophical question of Bizi history: is the machine divine, ancestral, instructional, or dangerous?

3. The Golden Empire

Centuries later, Justine, Theo, and Beli attempt to restore the old Bizi world at the empire’s dazzling peak.

The empire cannot survive on steel alone. It needs purpose, armies, and a story strong enough to defend itself.

4. The Riot of Sparks

Constanti burns in rebellion. Justine nearly flees, and Theo forces him to choose rule over escape. Beli opens the gates to restore order.

This event establishes that Bizi’s machinery and imperial glory do not prevent urban rebellion. The city can burn from within.

5. The Last Victories

Beli wins impossible campaigns and reclaims ancient territories against the Desert Coalition, Iron Tribes, and Sea Raiders. Each victory stretches the empire thinner.

Bizi expands and restores territory, but each success becomes another wound the empire inherits.

6. The Age of Focus

After decline and exhaustion, Focus seizes power and promises order through absolute efficiency.

Focus treats compassion as inefficient and progress as the higher goal. This marks one of Bizi’s most severe turns toward soulless system-rule.

7. The Great Invasion

The eastern empire collapses. Factories fall, shrines are destroyed, and the Bizi face extinction under Khosar the Conqueror.

Focus insists systems can fail and restart, but Khosar threatens to melt the systems entirely. Bizi enters an existential crisis.

8. Hera’s Counterattack

When victory seems impossible, Hera rises, marches into enemy territory, and turns one victory into ten.

Hera becomes the face of Bizi endurance. Machines break and empires fail, but determination endures.

9. The Three Titans

Followers of Machina, Melech, and Meca fight over creation, guidance, and control. Iro, Leon, and Nike confront the cult leaders as Titan faith becomes civil danger.

Bizi’s religious unity fractures around competing Titan interpretations.

10. The Schism

The Titan faith fractures permanently. Old allies become enemies, and the empire weakens from certainty within.

Archon Severus argues that unity built on compromise is rust. Theo’s disciples warn that certainty itself may be the greatest enemy of an empire.

11. The Restoration

The empire nearly collapses until Xios reforms the economy, repairs the military, and revives Bizi industry.

Xios frames Bizi’s survival through repair rather than replacement. The machine is damaged, not destroyed.

12. The Last Gear

Constanti falls under siege by the Iron Sultan. Machina’s engines fail, the Titans fall silent, and divided Bizi fight side by side so their knowledge survives.

The Defenders of Constanti face the end of the old machine-faith. Kharon’s recording leaves Bizi’s final lesson: steel rusts, cities fall, but ideas are eternal.
