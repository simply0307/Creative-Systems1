Sheen Timeline: The Rise and Trials of the Sheen
1. The Iron Roots

The Sheen story begins under Emperor Blackthorn and the Obsidian Lords. They drain the forests through Iron Root outposts while starving villages suffer.

Leafen Gao begins a rebellion against this system. The Iron Roots are not merely infrastructure; they are extraction made physical.

2. The Verdant Uprising

Hushan joins Leafen Gao as the rebellion spreads against Ironbark, Thornclaw, and Rootlash.

The uprising becomes larger than one village or one grievance. The rebellion is framed through seed imagery: buried, endangered, but capable of rising.

3. The Fall of the Obsidian Throne

Leafen Gao, Hushan, and Leshan confront Blackthorn, Lord of Iron. Leshan and Dowan collapse the Iron Roots themselves, forcing Blackthorn into one last monstrous stand.

The Obsidian system falls at the root, not merely at the branch. This ends the first major era of extraction in Sheen history.

4. Beli, Living City

After the rebellion, the Sheen rebuild and found Beli, the Living City. They begin the Root Network while a natural blight threatens the new kingdom.

This event establishes Beli as a major Sheen center and introduces the Root Network as a defining achievement. The faction moves from rebellion into civilization-building.

5. The Root Network

Dowan expands Sheen botanical science through barriers, shields, greenhouses, and trade routes. The Ash Serpent burns the nodes of the network.

The Root Network becomes both a practical and symbolic system. It feeds, protects, and connects Sheen society, but it also creates vulnerabilities when attacked.

6. The Blooming Age

Reane and Hushan help lead the Sheen into a golden age of shelters, retreats, springs, and living greenhouses. A desert warlord, the Drought King, tests the border.

The Blooming Age establishes Sheen prosperity. The faction learns that survival is not only about storage, but about what can endure winter.

7. The Court of Blossoms

Political factions emerge in Beli. Den tries to preserve stability while Aime and Tang rise through the court.

This marks the beginning of Sheen’s internal political crisis. The gardens may bloom, but the court can still rot.

8. The Reformer

Tang sees inequality and stagnation within Sheen society. He introduces reforms that genuinely help the poor and wins sympathy against corrupt nobles such as Lord Goldroot.

Tang begins as a legitimate reformer. His critique of tradition is grounded in real injustice.

9. Thorned Crown

Tang centralizes power and creates the Thornblades. Ringan, a former ally, tries to stop the imbalance.

Tang’s reform becomes control. He argues that control is reform that cannot be bribed, but Ringan sees that it also cannot be questioned.

10. The War of Roots

Civil war burns the forests. Shelters are weaponized, and the Root Network collapses under Ashroot, Briafang, and Ironvine.

Sheen’s own infrastructure becomes part of the war. Roads built to feed cities now carry armies, forcing Dowan, Hushan, and Ringan to cut the roads to save the roots.

11. Fall of Thorn Mang

Tang realizes too late what his reforms became. He falls as healer, commander, and living thorn avatar.

Tang’s tragedy is that he wanted the kingdom to live, but mistook pain for pruning. His fall ends the era of the Thorned Crown.

12. The Green Era

After the civil war, Dowan creates a renewed system of Root Network infrastructure, shelters, healing, greenhouses, and balance.

The Green Era becomes Sheen’s postwar renewal. Dowan and Reane frame the lesson clearly: growth without harmony becomes overgrowth, while tradition without growth becomes ash.
