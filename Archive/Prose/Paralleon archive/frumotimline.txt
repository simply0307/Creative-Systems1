Frumo Timeline: The Last Tide
1. The Tax of the Tides

Frumo’s known revolutionary history begins under King Ludvik’s tribute system. His fleets bleed Ristus while royal collectors seize treasure from every captain and family.

Lafayette emerges against this system, arguing that the sea gives enough for all Frumo. The tide begins turning against the royal vaults.

2. Voices of Revolution

Hidden taverns and pirate dens fill with reformers. Mirabeau, Danton, Marat, Robespier, and Lafayette demand a freer kingdom.

The revolution begins as speech, conspiracy, and organizing. Coral Bay and other royal centers become targets of revolutionary pressure.

3. The Fall of Silver Shoals

Corsair Danton leads revolutionary captains against Ludvik’s treasure fortress at Silver Shoals. They defeat General Carnot and prove that common pirates can defeat royal formations.

This event breaks the aura of royal military superiority.

4. The Sunken Fortress

The capital floods with revolution. King Ludvik discovers that treasure can buy armies, but not loyalty.

Lafayette confronts Ludvik’s idea of royal responsibility. The monarchy loses control of the revolutionary flood.

5. The Trial of the King

The monarchy falls, but Frumo divides over whether Ludvik should be imprisoned, spared, or executed.

Robespier argues that no kingdom can be free while the crown still lives. Lafayette warns that justice must not become hunger with a blade. The revolution begins turning inward.

6. The Reign of the Revolution

Victory curdles into suspicion as Robespier hunts enemies of freedom. Marat opposes him as the revolution itself becomes dangerous.

This is Frumo’s terror phase. The revolution that overthrew tyranny begins using suspicion as a weapon.

7. The Hero of the Republic

As chaos spreads, Polea wins impossible naval battles, defeats Frumo-world invaders, and gives the republic hope.

Polea claims he has not come to rule the republic, but his victories make the republic need him.

8. Lord Commander

Polea saves the republic, then takes control of it as the Council of Captains yields more power to him each year.

Emergency command becomes permanent authority. The crisis learns to change names, and so does ambition.

9. The Frumo Empire

Polea crowns himself Lord Commander of All Frumo. There is no king, no council, no election, only command.

Polea believes he freed the Frumo from kings. His shadow reveals the contradiction: he gave them a commander instead.

10. The Hundred Fleets

Polea’s armadas dominate Reath’s oceans. Rival Frumo sea powers, including the Coral Crown, Kelpbound Princes, and Brasswater League, unite against his endless victories.

Frumo’s liberation has become an imperial sea power. Fear begins to sound like obedience.

11. The Frozen Sea

Polea attempts to conquer the northern oceans, but storms, ice, hunger, and the Ice Leviathan shatter the once-invincible navy.

This is Polea’s limit. No tide has ever refused him, but the Frozen Sea is not tide. It is silence.

12. The Last Tide

Former allies and rival fleets face Polea one final time. The Restored Council returns after his fall or defeat, carrying the uneasy knowledge that freedom can decay again.

Frumo survives the revolution, the terror, Polea’s rise, empire, and disaster. Its final lesson is that the fight for freedom does not end when kings fall.
