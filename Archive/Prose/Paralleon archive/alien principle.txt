# Alien Cosmology: The Underpinnings of Occurrence

The alien factions are not merely species, armies, or distant civilizations. They are the foundational pressures beneath reality. They are the invisible forces that shape why events happen the way they do. Every empire, revolution, invention, religion, plague, tournament, myth, betrayal, and cultural movement can be understood as a visible expression of these deeper alien principles.

They are the grammar of occurrence.

A mortal civilization might believe it is acting from tradition, law, hunger, fear, prophecy, ambition, or grief. But beneath those motives, alien influence gives the event its shape. Voltari turns life into structure. Xynarth turns pressure into multiplication. Zendra turns perception into reality. These forces are not always worshipped, named, or even understood by the people living under them, but they are always present.

This means history in the universe is never only political or cultural. It is metaphysical. A civil war is not just a civil war. It may be Voltari order collapsing under Xynarth pressure, or Zendra hallucination infecting a population until the people can no longer agree on what is true. A golden age is not simply prosperity. It may be a temporary alignment of alien forces, where structure, belief, and growth all point in the same direction before eventually splitting apart.

The mortal factions are local expressions of these universal pressures. Rumin, Sheen, Bizi, Frumo, Indela, and the other civilizations each interpret the alien grammar through their own bodies, governments, economies, art, and myths. They are historical masks worn by cosmic forces.

Rumin expresses alien order through law, debt, conquest, and civic spectacle. Sheen expresses it through dynasty, memory, medicine, and ritual preservation. Bizi expresses it through sacred infrastructure, rails, holy fuel, and the worship of systems. Frumo expresses it through grief, revolution, freezing trauma, and the dangerous afterlife of righteous anger. Each faction believes itself unique, and culturally it is. But beneath culture, the same alien principles repeat in new configurations.

This is why the universe has a repeating, cascading quality. History does not repeat because people are stupid. It repeats because the same alien forces keep reappearing through different materials. A rail empire, a forest dynasty, a financial republic, and a frozen revolution may seem unrelated, but each can be read as a different answer to the same cosmic pressures.

The alien factions define what can happen.

The mortal factions define how it feels when it happens.
