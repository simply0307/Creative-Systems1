## Core question

What happens when more becomes unstoppable?

Zynarth is not merely insects, monsters, or biological horror. It is the logic of accumulation: repetition, compounding advantage, crowd force, appetite, survival, and collective action.

## Constructive expression

Resilience, adaptation, solidarity, and the ability of many small actors to challenge concentrated power.

## Failure mode

Need becomes appetite. Growth continues because growth worked before. Responsibility is distributed until no one feels accountable for the flood.

## Mechanical vocabulary

Multiply, compound, consume, recover, spread, recruit, overwhelm, and overextend.
