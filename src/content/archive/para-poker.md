## Product premise

Para Poker should use poker's durable competitive meanings—reads, pressure, incomplete information, identity, history, and rivalry—without importing deposits, wagering, rake, cash-equivalent chips, or paid competitive power.

## Current caution

The archived competitive circuit requires paid-only K-XP for major qualification and makes unsupported claims of legal safety. That document is a risky product-strategy artifact, not an implementation specification.
