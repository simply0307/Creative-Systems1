## Provisional role

Reath currently functions as the safest scope for Rumin, Frumo, Bizi, Sheen, their characters, timelines, and locations. This prevents those materials from silently becoming ecosystem-wide foundation canon.

Its relationship to Paralleon, Para, and EGGS still requires a user decision.
