## Concept kernel

Frumo is an emotional weather system. A wounded people build solidarity through remembered injustice, then discover that institutions fed by pain require the wound to remain open.

Its strongest reusable question is: **how long can a people live on righteous grief before grief becomes another throne?**

## Rewrite boundary

Preserve frozen futurity, grief as legitimacy, tribunals, revolutionary purity, and fear of healing. Replace the French Revolutionary sequence and one-to-one historical figures.
