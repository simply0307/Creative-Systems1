## Skill test

The player must know when to convert momentum and when further growth becomes overextension.

Healthy versions expose thresholds, costs, and counterplay. A mechanic that merely makes the current leader increasingly impossible to challenge expresses Zynarth's failure mode without becoming a good game system.
