## Role

Astral Vanguards are travelers, scouts, historians, archivists, preservers, witnesses, and intruders. Unlike the alien principles, they do not pressure reality from beneath. They move across expressions and learn to recognize the pattern.

## Central tension

Does seeing the pattern create responsibility—or the most dangerous kind of confidence? Saving, preserving, and interpreting can all become forms of control when consent disappears.
