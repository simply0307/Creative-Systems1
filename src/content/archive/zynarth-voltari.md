## Synthesis

A swarm learns bureaucracy. Consumption becomes procedural. Growth acquires standards, routes, ledgers, and enforcement.

Its constructive possibility is coordinated scale: many actors sharing reliable infrastructure. Its destructive form is extraction so organized that no individual action appears responsible.

## Mechanical direction

Engine building, production chains, formation swarms, escalating economies, and the tension between efficient growth and overextension.
