## Working definition

A Morphling gives a convergence a body, identity, group, or stable form. Morphlings are not generic shapeshifters, miscellaneous mutants, or arbitrary mixtures.

Every Morphling should identify its source convergence, embodiment logic, stable and variable traits, narrative role, and mechanical affordance.

## Why they matter

They prevent the system from becoming a chart of pure teams. They make translation, contradiction, inheritance, and mixed belonging visible.
