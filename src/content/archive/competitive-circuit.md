## Why it remains in the archive

This proposal records an important product experiment and the tensions it revealed. It should be preserved as strategy history, not silently discarded.

## Why it must not guide implementation

K-XP from paid events is required for major qualification, paid participation becomes a proxy for legitimacy, and the document calls the structure legally clean without qualified review. Those elements contradict the player-first direction.
