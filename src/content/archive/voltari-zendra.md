## Synthesis

Dream becomes law. Interpretation acquires protocol. A shared symbolic world is made reproducible through ritual, architecture, education, or interface.

Its constructive possibility is common meaning maintained carefully. Its destructive form is propaganda that no longer looks like persuasion because it has become infrastructure.

## Mechanical direction

Rule prediction, ritual sequences, declared information, controlled reveals, and systems that reward reading how others interpret the same signal.
