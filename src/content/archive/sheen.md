## Concept kernel

Sheen imagines history as rings in wood: continuity makes life bearable, but inherited memory can become a cage that teaches each new branch how it is permitted to grow.

Its strongest reusable question is: **what do we owe the past, and when does that debt become a cage?**

## Distinction

Sheen remembers, inherits, heals, legitimizes, and preserves. Its identity should not collapse into Bizi's routing and infrastructure systems.
