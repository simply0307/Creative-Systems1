## Concept kernel

Bizi treats maintenance as prayer and movement as participation in a sacred system. Whoever controls the route, fuel, or Hub controls access and legitimacy.

Its strongest reusable question is: **when a society depends on a system completely, can it afford to tell the truth about that system?**

## Distinction

Bizi routes, powers, connects, repairs, schedules, and creates dependency. Its identity should not collapse into Sheen's memory and inheritance systems.
